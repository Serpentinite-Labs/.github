## About Us

Serpentinite Labs develops software, artificial intelligence solutions, and digital tools that help geoscientists work faster, make better decisions, and unlock new opportunities across Earth science disciplines.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between cutting-edge technologies and established geoscience workflows. We believe that advances in artificial intelligence, automation, data science, and visualization should be accessible, practical, and seamlessly integrated into the daily work of geoscientists.

Founded by geoscientists, researchers, and software developers working at the intersection of Earth sciences and artificial intelligence.

## Featured Projects

### G-Spyke

A mobile application designed for geoscientists to create geological columns directly in the field.

G-Spyke streamlines field data collection, reduces manual processing, and enables rapid digitalization of geological observations.

### Autonomous Geoscience Agents *(Coming Soon)*

We are developing a new generation of autonomous AI agents designed to assist geoscientists with research, interpretation, data processing, and decision-making workflows.

## Vision

We envision a future where geoscientists can leverage artificial intelligence as naturally as they use maps, GIS platforms, modeling software, or field notebooks today.

By combining scientific expertise with modern software engineering and AI, we aim to build tools that make geoscience more efficient, accessible, and impactful.
